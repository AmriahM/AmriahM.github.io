Introduction

Stuff-Iced

Looking for a refreshing snack on a hot Californian day? Well look no furthur! From classic favorites to unique flavors, we serve delicious 100% vegan ice cream that everyone can enjoy. Whether you're vegan or just curious, there's a scoop waiting for you!

Target audience

Stuff-Iced targets people of all ages who enjoy ice cream and are looking for delicious plant-based alternatives. This includes vegans, people with dietary restrictions, environmentally conscious consumers, and anyone interested in trying something new.

The primary goal of our target audience is to find a place where they can enjoy a variety of flavorful ice cream options without the use of animal products. The website should make it easy to explore flavors, learn about ingredients, and find information about the brand.

About Us

At Stuff-Iced, we believe that everyone deserves a little something sweet! Our goal is to provide a welcoming space where people of all ages can come together and enjoy delicious ice cream.

Whether you're stopping by with friends, spending time with family, or simply treating yourself, we're happy to have you. We offer a wide selection of flavors and we're 100% vegan, so there's something for everyone to enjoy!
